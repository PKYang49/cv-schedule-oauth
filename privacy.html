Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Purpose

CV Schedule Mailer is a private, single-user automation used by its account owner to send a preconfigured monthly schedule email through the Gmail API.

Google user data accessed

The application requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send scope. It uses this permission solely to send messages explicitly prepared by the account owner's local automation. It does not read, search, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Storage and retention

  • OAuth credentials are stored locally on the account owner's computer with owner-only file permissions.
  • Limited delivery metadata—send time, Gmail message ID, configured recipients, and attachment filename—may be retained locally to prevent duplicate sends.
  • No Gmail inbox content, advertising profile, or patient data is collected.

Sharing and transfer

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transferred for any purpose other than the Gmail API operation initiated by the account owner.

Security and revocation

Credentials are restricted to the minimum required scope. The account owner may revoke access at any time from the Google Account security settings and may delete locally stored credentials.
