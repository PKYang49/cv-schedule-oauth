Private scheduling utility

CV Schedule Mailer

CV Schedule Mailer is a single-user automation that sends a preconfigured monthly cardiovascular schedule email from the account owner's Gmail account.

Limited Google access

The application requests one permission only:

gmail.send
  • It can send the schedule message and attachment selected by the account owner.
  • It cannot read, search, modify, or delete Gmail messages.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.

How it works

The automation runs on the account owner's computer. It creates a message from locally configured recipients and a locally available schedule file, then submits that message through the Gmail API.

Data handling

No patient data or Gmail inbox content is collected. OAuth credentials and limited delivery metadata remain on the account owner's computer and are not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
